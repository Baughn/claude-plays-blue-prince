## Reasoning (Day 2)

- A: W,U true, B false ⇒ prize ∈ {black, white}; a 2nd wind-up key exists in the room.
- B: W,U false, B true ⇒ prize in BLUE; no 2nd wind-up key in the room.
Decider: is there a second wind-up key anywhere in the room (besides the desk one)?
- Searched the room: only ONE wind-up key (on the desk, "A rather curious Wind-Up Key — a component of the Parlor Game"). ⇒ Case B ⇒ chose **BLUE**.
